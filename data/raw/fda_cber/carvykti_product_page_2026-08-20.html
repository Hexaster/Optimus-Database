CARVYKTI

  • Cellular & Gene Therapy Products
    • Approved Cellular and Gene Therapy Products

STN:125746 
Proper Name: ciltacabtagene autoleucel
Tradename: CARVYKTI
Manufacturer: Janssen Biotech, Inc.
Indications: 

  • Treatment of adult patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma who have received at least 1 prior line of therapy, including a proteasome inhibitor and an immunomodulatory agent, and are refractory to lenalidomide.
